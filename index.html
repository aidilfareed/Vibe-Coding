About Our Event

Bringing communities together through great food and greater purpose

Our annual charity pizza event is more than just a fundraiser—it's a celebration of community, generosity, and the joy of giving back. We partner with local pizzerias to create mouth-watering pizzas that will satisfy your cravings while supporting an incredible cause.

All proceeds from this event go directly to Adventure Quest World Club, helping provide educational resources, adventure programs, and life-changing experiences for children who need them most.

Adventure Quest World Club

Adventure Quest World Club is a non-profit organization dedicated to providing educational adventures and life-changing experiences for children from underserved communities. Through outdoor activities, cultural exchanges, and educational programs, they help young people develop confidence, leadership skills, and a global perspective.

Your support through this pizza fundraiser helps fund:

  • Adventure camps and outdoor education
  • Educational materials and resources
  • Transportation for field trips
  • Scholarships for children in need
