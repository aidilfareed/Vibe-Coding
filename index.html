About Our Charity Event

Making a positive impact in our community through delicious pizza

Our Mission

Lions Club is dedicated to serving our community and helping those in need. This pizza event helps fund our local outreach programs, vision screenings, and youth development initiatives.

Premium Quality

We serve only the finest quality pizza made with premium ingredients. Every slice is crafted with care and love, ensuring a delightful experience for all ages.

Community Impact

100% of proceeds go directly to community programs including food drives, educational scholarships, and assistance for families in need. Your purchase makes a real difference!
